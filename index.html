What HomeTrust does

HomeTrust Zambia is an online platform and mobile app. It helps households in Lusaka find verified domestic workers, and helps workers find steady jobs. Customers pay workers directly for each job, in cash or mobile money. HomeTrust does not take payment for the job itself.

HomeTrust earns from three things: prepaid job credit that workers buy, optional customer memberships, and a one-off placement fee when an employer hires a full-time worker. These fees are paid in the app, and the payments are processed by our payment provider, Lenco.

How it works

1. Choose

Pick a service and see verified workers who are available, nearest first, with their rating.

2. Book

Choose the time and how long, and add instructions. You see one final charge before you send the request, and the worker accepts. The minimum booking is 3 hours.

3. Follow arrival

After the worker accepts, you see their distance and arrival time. You pay the worker directly when the visit ends, then rate the visit.

Services: nanny, babysitter, cleaner, cook and housekeeper. Looking for a full-time worker? HomeTrust shortlists verified workers for you.

Six checks before anyone is listed

A worker appears to customers only when HomeTrust has cleared every check.

Identity and photo

A clear photo of the worker and their NRC.

References

Previous employers are contacted.

Police clearance

Checked and renewed every 12 months.

Medical clearance

Checked and renewed every 12 months.

Skills assessment

A practical assessment of the work they will do.

Interview

Conduct, language and expectations.

Workers' documents are visible only to authorised HomeTrust staff. Your address and phone number are shared with a worker only after they accept your booking. Location is shared only during a booking. If something goes wrong, the app has an SOS button and a way to report a problem. HomeTrust records every complaint and can warn or suspend a worker. Verification is not insurance and does not guarantee that incidents cannot happen.

For workers

Registering is free. You choose the services you offer, upload your documents, and HomeTrust verifies you. Once you are verified, customers near you can book you, and they pay you directly. You buy job credit from HomeTrust to take jobs. Your profile photo must show you. Any other picture leads to suspension, and a later change needs HomeTrust's approval.

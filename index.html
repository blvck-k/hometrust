How it works

1. Choose

Pick the help you need and see verified workers who are available, nearest first.

2. Book

Set the time and how long. You see one clear price before you send your request.

3. Welcome them in

Follow their arrival on the map. Pay the worker directly when the visit ends.

Why families trust HomeTrust

Every worker is checked by our team before a family ever sees their name.

  • Identity and a clear photo
  • References from past employers
  • Police clearance
  • Medical clearance
  • A practical skills assessment
  • A personal interview

Your address is shared only after a worker accepts your booking. Location is shared only during the visit. The app has an SOS button and a way to report any problem, and we follow up on every report.

For domestic workers

Joining is free. Choose the work you offer, upload your documents, and once you are verified, families near you can book you and pay you directly.

Simple and fair

Families pay no booking fee. Workers buy prepaid job credit, and families can choose an optional membership to see more workers. Employers who hire a full-time worker through us pay a one-off placement fee. See our Terms for the details.

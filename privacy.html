1. Who is responsible for your data

HomeTrust Zambia, based in Lusaka, decides how your personal data is used. Contact: [EMAIL ADDRESS].

2. What we collect

  • Everyone: name, phone number, and the code we send to confirm it.
  • Customers: the address where the work happens, instructions you write, and for care services any special needs you describe.
  • Workers: a photo, NRC, references, police and medical clearance documents, the services you offer, and interview and assessment results.
  • Location: a worker's location while available and during a booking, so customers can see who is nearest and follow arrival, and so check-in can be confirmed.
  • Payments to HomeTrust: the amount, date and reference of job credit purchases, memberships and placement fees. Card numbers and mobile money PINs are handled by our payment provider, Lenco, and are not stored by us.
  • Use of the app: bookings, ratings, complaints and messages to support.

3. Why we use it

  • To create accounts, show available workers, and carry out bookings.
  • To verify workers and keep customers and workers safe, including SOS and complaints.
  • To take payments for job credit, memberships and placement fees, and keep records.
  • To contact you about your bookings and account, and to improve the service.

4. Who sees it

  • A customer's address and phone number are shared with a worker only after the worker accepts the booking.
  • A worker's documents are seen only by the worker and authorised HomeTrust staff. Customers see the worker's photo, services, rating and verification status.
  • Our payment provider, Lenco, processes payments to us. Our hosting and technology providers store data on our behalf.
  • We share data with the police or other authorities when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety.
  • We do not sell personal data.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account and booking records while your account is open and for [period, for example 5 years] afterwards where the law or our accounts require it. Worker documents are deleted [period] after a worker leaves, unless we must keep them. Location is kept for [period] after a booking ends.

6. Security

Documents are stored securely and only authorised staff can open them. Staff access is logged. No system is completely safe, so please keep your login details private.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it where the law allows. You can turn off location in your phone settings, but workers cannot be listed or complete jobs without it. Write to [EMAIL ADDRESS].

8. Children

The platform is for people aged 18 and over. Customers may describe a child's care needs in a booking. Please share only what the worker needs to look after the child safely.

9. Changes and contact

We will tell you in the app if we change this policy. Questions or complaints: [EMAIL ADDRESS], Lusaka.

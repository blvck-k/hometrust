1. Who we are

HomeTrust Zambia is run by HomeTrust Zambia, based in Lusaka. In these terms, "HomeTrust", "we" and "us" mean HomeTrust Zambia. "You" means anyone who uses our app or website.

2. What the platform does

HomeTrust is a platform that connects households ("customers") with domestic workers ("workers"). We verify workers before they are listed. We do not employ workers, and we do not take payment for jobs.

3. Accounts

  • You must give true information and keep your login details private.
  • You must be 18 or older to create an account.
  • We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms or puts others at risk.

4. Bookings and payment for jobs

  • Customers choose an available worker, a time and a length. The minimum booking is 3 hours. The app shows one final charge before the customer sends the request.
  • The worker accepts or declines. A customer can cancel before the worker accepts, at no charge.
  • The customer pays the worker the final charge directly, in cash or mobile money, when the visit ends. HomeTrust does not collect, hold or refund job payments.
  • If there is a problem with a job, report it in the app. We record the complaint, speak to both sides, and may warn or suspend the worker. We cannot refund a job payment.

5. Workers

  • Registering is free. A worker is listed only after HomeTrust clears six checks: identity and photo, references, police clearance, medical clearance, skills assessment and an interview. Police and medical clearances are renewed every 12 months.
  • The profile photo must show the worker. A photo of anything else leads to suspension. A new photo must be approved by HomeTrust, and the old photo stays until then.
  • Workers buy prepaid job credit from HomeTrust. For example, K1,000 of job credit costs K100. When a job is completed, its value is used from the credit. A worker needs enough credit to accept a job and is hidden from customers when credit is too low.
  • [Confirm: Job credit is not refundable once bought, except when a payment was made in error.]
  • If a worker does not check out, the job is counted automatically one hour after the booked end time.
  • Workers must arrive on time, behave respectfully, and keep customers' homes and information private.

6. Memberships

  • Customers can choose an optional paid membership (Plus or Premium). A member sees more available workers to choose from. Membership does not include free labour. The customer still pays the worker for every job.
  • A membership lasts one month and does not renew by itself. The customer pays again to renew.
  • A customer can cancel within 7 days of joining and get a full refund. After 7 days, memberships are not refunded.

7. Full-time placement

  • HomeTrust acts as an agent that helps an employer find a full-time worker. [Confirm that the agency permits and licences required in Zambia are in place.]
  • The employer pays a one-off placement fee of K350 when they confirm a hire, after the worker has accepted. The fee is not refundable.
  • Workers are never charged for a placement. The salary is agreed and paid directly between the employer and the worker.

8. Payments to HomeTrust

Payments for job credit, memberships and placement fees are made by mobile money or card and processed by our payment provider, Lenco. We do not store card numbers or mobile money PINs. Provider charges, if any, are shown before you pay.

9. Safety, location and conduct

  • Location is shared during a booking so the customer can follow the worker's arrival and so check-in can be confirmed. It stops when the visit ends.
  • The app has an SOS button and a way to report a problem. In a real emergency, contact the police or emergency services first.
  • Do not use the platform to harass, threaten, discriminate against or cheat anyone, or to arrange work outside the platform to avoid its fees.

10. Our responsibility

We take care in verifying workers, but verification is not insurance and does not guarantee that incidents cannot happen. To the extent the law allows, HomeTrust is not responsible for the actions of customers or workers, or for losses that arise from a job. Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under Zambian law that cannot be limited.

11. Changes and ending

We may update these terms and will tell you in the app. If you keep using the platform after a change, you accept it. You can close your account at any time.
